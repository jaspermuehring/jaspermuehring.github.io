Investment Intelligence System (IIS)

A personal investment research system for organizing evidence, comparing research, and maintaining a history of research information.

IIS runs locally on Windows. It is not an automated trading service and does not place or execute trades.

Google integration

Google account identification verifies the account authorized to use the integration. Access to explicitly selected Google Drive documents supports research information management.

IIS retrieves only documents included in its local retrieval allowlist. Its Google document integration is read-only: it does not upload, edit, or delete Google documents. Selected documents may be preserved as local historical snapshots.

Data handling

Google authorization credentials are protected locally using Windows DPAPI. Document snapshots and other local research data have separate storage protections. Selected research context may be processed by an AI provider during an explicitly authorized live research run.

Read the privacy policy for details about storage, provider processing, authorization revocation, and local deletion.
